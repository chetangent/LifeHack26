# Demo Narrative

## Demo Goal
Show judges that AgentShelf can take an ordinary product catalog, transform it into AI-ready content, and prove the improvement through measurable recommendation performance.

## Demo Setup
Use a small running-shoe catalog with:
- 10 SKUs
- existing title
- price
- short description
- bullet features

Highlight one weak product page and one strong transformed result.

## Talk Track
### 1. Open with the shift
"Commerce is moving from search boxes to conversations. But most product content is still written for websites, not AI agents."

### 2. Show the raw catalog problem
Display a typical SKU:
- generic title
- a few specs
- vague marketing copy

Say:
"An AI assistant cannot confidently recommend this for a runner training in Singapore's humidity on a S$200 budget, because the product page never states those decision points clearly."

### 3. Upload the catalog
Show:
- CSV upload
- category selection: Running Shoes
- processing state

### 4. Reveal the transformed output
Open one product and show:
- AI summary
- personas
- use cases
- strengths
- tradeoffs
- structured tags

Say:
"We convert static product copy into recommendation-ready intelligence."

### 5. Show the readiness score
Display:
- overall score
- score breakdown
- missing fields

Example narration:
"This product scores 52 out of 100 because it lacks climate suitability, fit guidance, and comparison signals. The system tells the brand exactly what to improve."

### 6. Run simulation prompts
Use three prompts:
- "I need lightweight running shoes for humid weather under S$200."
- "I am a beginner training for my first half marathon."
- "I want the best value daily trainer for hot climates."

Show:
- whether the product is selected
- confidence score
- why it wins or loses

### 7. Apply suggested improvements
Regenerate or accept the recommended content improvements.

Say:
"Now we add the missing signals AI agents actually use in reasoning."

### 8. Re-run simulation
Show the score rise and higher selection rate.

Example:
- readiness score: 52 -> 81
- simulation win rate: 1/3 -> 3/3

### 9. Land the value
"This is how brands move from being present in a catalog to being chosen in an AI conversation."

## Suggested Demo Flow Timing
- Problem framing: 30 seconds
- Raw SKU walkthrough: 30 seconds
- Upload and generation: 45 seconds
- Readiness scoring: 45 seconds
- Simulation before/after: 60 seconds
- Closing impact: 30 seconds

Total:
- about 4 minutes

## Judge-Friendly Soundbites
- "SEO made brands discoverable on web pages. AgentShelf makes them recommendable in AI answers."
- "We do not just generate copy. We generate machine-usable product reasoning."
- "Our score is actionable because it is tied to simulation outcomes, not vanity metrics."

## What To Emphasize
- clear problem-to-solution fit
- measurable outcome
- practical value for brands
- scalable architecture beyond one category

## If Judges Ask About Defensibility
Answer:
"Our moat is the combination of category-aware schemas, recommendation scoring logic, and simulation data that teaches brands what AI systems actually need in order to recommend products."

## If Judges Ask About Feasibility
Answer:
"The MVP is feasible because it starts with narrow-category enrichment on top of existing catalogs using structured templates, LLM generation, and a rules-based scoring layer."
