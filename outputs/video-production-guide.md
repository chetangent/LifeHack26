# Video Production Guide

## Goal

Record a 3 to 4 minute demo that makes AgentShelf feel like a real product with a measurable outcome.

## Core story

The video should answer one question:

Why would a brand care?

The answer is:

Because AI shopping assistants will not recommend products confidently unless the catalog gives them the right signals.

## Best recording flow

1. Open on the landing page and state the problem in one sentence.
2. Show the weakest SKU and say why it fails.
3. Show the missing recommendation signals.
4. Click `Apply suggested fixes`.
5. Show the AI-ready profile.
6. Show the score uplift.
7. Show the simulation before and after.
8. End with the one-line pitch.

## Best on-screen sequence

- Hero section
- Guided flow banner
- Weakest SKU panel
- Before content
- After content
- Score breakdown
- Prompt simulation

## What to say

Use this structure:

- "Shoppers are moving from search to AI conversations."
- "Brands still write content for websites, not for AI recommendation."
- "AgentShelf finds weak SKUs, improves their content, and proves the lift."
- "Here is a product that starts weak."
- "Here are the missing recommendation signals."
- "Now we apply the fixes."
- "Now the score and recommendation confidence improve."

## Keep these numbers on screen

- baseline score
- improved score
- prompt wins before
- prompt wins after

## Recording tips

- use one hero SKU for the full video
- avoid switching between too many products
- keep cursor movement slow and intentional
- zoom the browser if needed so text is readable
- rehearse the timing before recording

## Strong final line

"As commerce shifts from search results to AI answers, brands need content that is not just searchable, but recommendable."
