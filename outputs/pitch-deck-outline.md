# Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Title
**AgentShelf**
Make products recommendation-ready for AI shopping assistants

Subtitle:
From static catalog copy to structured, contextual, AI-readable product intelligence

## Slide 2: Problem
Digital commerce content was built for humans browsing websites.

AI shopping is changing the interface:
- users ask intent-rich questions
- agents compare options and constraints
- products must be machine-understandable to be recommended

Most brands are not ready because their catalogs lack:
- context
- persona fit
- tradeoffs
- machine-readable structure

## Slide 3: Why Now
- conversational shopping is growing fast
- AI assistants are becoming a discovery layer
- brands risk invisibility if their content is not AI-readable
- current SEO and PDP optimization tools do not solve recommendation readiness

## Slide 4: User Pain
Brand teams cannot easily answer:
- why one SKU gets recommended and another does not
- what an AI agent still needs to know before recommending a product
- how to scale richer content across thousands of SKUs
- how to measure if content is "AI-ready"

## Slide 5: Solution
AgentShelf is an AI content copilot plus readiness engine.

It:
- ingests existing product catalogs
- generates structured and persona-aware content
- scores AI readiness
- simulates real shopper prompts to expose content gaps

## Slide 6: Product Walkthrough
Show four modules:
- Catalog Ingestion
- AI Content Copilot
- Readiness Score
- Simulation Lab

Use a single SKU example through all four steps.

## Slide 7: Example Transformation
Before:
- title
- bullet specs
- generic marketing copy

After:
- structured recommendation schema
- persona and use-case narratives
- tradeoffs and comparisons
- recommendation reasons
- gap alerts and suggested fixes

## Slide 8: Scoring Framework
AI Readiness Score out of 100

Example dimensions:
- facts and attributes
- contextual richness
- persona coverage
- use-case coverage
- differentiation
- trust evidence
- machine readability

## Slide 9: Simulation Lab
Run the same product against natural language prompts.

Show:
- selection rate
- confidence score
- failure reasons
- content gap recommendations

Example:
"Only 22% win rate for humid-weather running queries due to missing breathability and climate tags."

## Slide 10: Why We Win
- not just copy generation
- not just search optimization
- built specifically for AI recommendation pipelines
- measurable and iterative
- works with existing catalogs instead of requiring brands to start over

## Slide 11: Business Impact
- improved AI discovery visibility
- higher recommendation likelihood
- faster enrichment across large catalogs
- stronger conversion from conversational commerce channels

## Slide 12: MVP and Roadmap
### MVP
- one category
- CSV upload
- content generation
- readiness scoring
- prompt simulation dashboard

### Roadmap
- API integrations with CMS and marketplaces
- multilingual support
- auto-generated comparison matrices
- live performance feedback from AI referral traffic

## Slide 13: Closing
As commerce shifts from search results to AI answers, brands need to optimize not just for clicks, but for recommendation.

**AgentShelf helps products speak the language of AI.**
