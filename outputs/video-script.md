# Demo Video Script

## Goal

Record a 3 to 4 minute demo that makes the project feel inevitable, useful, and measurable.

## Video structure

### 1. Hook
Time:
0:00 to 0:20

Script:
"Commerce is moving from search boxes to AI conversations. But most brands still write product content for websites, not for AI assistants that need to recommend products with confidence."

On screen:
- app landing page
- title and value proposition

### 2. Problem
Time:
0:20 to 0:45

Script:
"A shopper no longer types a keyword like 'running shoes size 10.' They ask something like, 'I'm training for a half marathon in Singapore's humid weather and need lightweight shoes under S$200.' Most catalogs do not contain enough structured, contextual, decision-ready information for an AI assistant to answer that well."

On screen:
- example shopper prompt
- raw product row or basic product page

### 3. Introduce AgentShelf
Time:
0:45 to 1:10

Script:
"We built AgentShelf, a platform that helps brands transform normal product catalogs into AI-ready product intelligence. It enriches product content, scores recommendation readiness, and simulates whether AI assistants would actually choose a product."

On screen:
- readiness dashboard
- product list

### 4. Show the weak baseline
Time:
1:10 to 1:40

Script:
"Here's a product with ordinary catalog content. It has a title, a price, and a few features, but it doesn't clearly express climate fit, buyer persona, training use case, or tradeoffs. That means the AI assistant lacks key reasoning signals."

On screen:
- one selected SKU
- raw attributes
- low or medium readiness state

### 5. Enrichment
Time:
1:40 to 2:10

Script:
"AgentShelf turns that raw content into an AI-ready profile: a structured summary, recommended personas, use cases, strengths, tradeoffs, and machine-readable tags. This gives AI systems the context they need to compare and recommend products."

On screen:
- enriched product output
- side-by-side before vs after

### 6. Scoring
Time:
2:10 to 2:35

Script:
"We then score the product for AI readiness across completeness, contextual richness, persona coverage, trust signals, and machine readability. Instead of saying 'improve your copy,' we tell the brand exactly what is missing."

On screen:
- score breakdown
- missing signals

### 7. Simulation
Time:
2:35 to 3:05

Script:
"The most important part is simulation. We test the product against realistic shopping prompts and show whether it is selected, how confident the recommendation is, and what content gaps caused it to lose."

On screen:
- prompt list
- matched vs missed
- confidence numbers

### 8. Before and after impact
Time:
3:05 to 3:30

Script:
"After applying the suggested improvements, the product's readiness score goes up and its simulation win rate improves. This gives brands a measurable path to becoming recommendable in conversational commerce."

On screen:
- score increase
- win-rate improvement

### 9. Close
Time:
3:30 to 3:50

Script:
"SEO made brands discoverable on webpages. AgentShelf makes them recommendable in AI answers."

On screen:
- final product dashboard
- product tagline

## Recording tips

- Keep the pace fast.
- Use one hero SKU throughout the demo.
- Avoid showing too many features.
- Emphasize measurable before-and-after improvement.
- Speak to business value, not just technical novelty.

## Minimum assets to prepare before recording

- one polished hero screen
- one product detail view
- one readiness score breakdown
- one simulation before-and-after result

## Optional ending line

"As AI becomes the new storefront, brands need to optimize not just for clicks, but for recommendation."
